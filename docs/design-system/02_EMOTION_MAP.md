# Emotion Map

Before designing or implementing any page in Ascend AI, we define its intended emotional impact. This ensures that the aesthetic and interactive choices align with the user's psychological state at that moment in their journey.

## Why an Emotion Map?

A user checking their macros feels different from a user celebrating a 30-day streak. By defining the *emotion* first, we dictate the *design choices* (color density, typography size, motion style) consistently and intentionally, avoiding arbitrary flair.

---

### Dashboard (Mission Control)
- **Intended Emotion**: Clarity & Control
- **Emotional Intensity**: Low/Calm
- **User Should Feel**: "I know exactly where I stand today."
- **Primary User Action**: Glance and log quick actions.
- **Motion Style**: Subtle fades. No bouncy animations.
- **Typography Style**: High contrast, easily scannable metrics.
- **Color Usage**: Minimal. The Indigo accent (`#4F46E5`) is used sparingly for primary actions. Backgrounds are strictly dark (`#0F172A`).
- **Content Density**: Comfortable. Lots of breathing room.
- **Microinteraction Style**: Sharp and immediate.

### Workout 
- **Intended Emotion**: Focus & Intensity
- **Emotional Intensity**: High
- **User Should Feel**: "I am in the zone."
- **Primary User Action**: Complete sets and track volume.
- **Motion Style**: Energetic, snappy transitions.
- **Typography Style**: Large, bold numbers (Inter Display).
- **Color Usage**: Purple accent (`#A855F7`) dominates active states. 
- **Content Density**: Compact. Data must be dense but legible mid-exercise.
- **Microinteraction Style**: High-feedback (haptic-ready), distinct pressed states.

### Nutrition
- **Intended Emotion**: Precision & Accountability
- **Emotional Intensity**: Medium
- **User Should Feel**: "I am hitting my targets."
- **Primary User Action**: Log meals and check macros.
- **Motion Style**: Smooth, fluid progress bar fills.
- **Typography Style**: Tabular numbers for macros to ensure alignment.
- **Color Usage**: Green accent (`#22C55E`) for success states and progress.
- **Content Density**: Default. Balance between data density and readability.
- **Microinteraction Style**: Satisfying progress ring completions.

### Progress & Reports (Analytics)
- **Intended Emotion**: Reflection & Insight
- **Emotional Intensity**: Low
- **User Should Feel**: "I am making steady, measurable progress."
- **Primary User Action**: Analyze charts and trends.
- **Motion Style**: Elegant chart drawing animations on load.
- **Typography Style**: Hierarchy-driven. Small labels, clear tooltips.
- **Color Usage**: Orange accent (`#F97316`) for trend lines. Multi-color ONLY for comparative charts.
- **Content Density**: High. Maximize data-to-ink ratio.
- **Microinteraction Style**: Smooth hover reveals on charts.

### Hall of Progress (Gamification)
- **Intended Emotion**: Pride & Motivation
- **Emotional Intensity**: Very High
- **User Should Feel**: "My hard work is paying off."
- **Primary User Action**: Review achievements and streaks.
- **Motion Style**: Celebratory. Badges scale up, streaks pulse.
- **Typography Style**: Bold, serif or high-contrast display fonts for achievement titles.
- **Color Usage**: Gold accent (`#EAB308`). This is the *only* place Gold is used dominantly.
- **Content Density**: Comfortable. Let achievements breathe.
- **Microinteraction Style**: Confetti-like, glow effects on hover.

### AI Coach
- **Intended Emotion**: Support & Intelligence
- **Emotional Intensity**: Medium
- **User Should Feel**: "I have an expert guiding me."
- **Primary User Action**: Ask questions, receive insights.
- **Motion Style**: Organic. "Thinking" indicators should feel fluid, almost breathing.
- **Typography Style**: Highly legible body text for long-form reading.
- **Color Usage**: Cyan accent (`#06B6D4`). Subtle glowing borders on AI-generated cards.
- **Content Density**: Default. Focus on reading comfort.
- **Microinteraction Style**: Typewriter or smooth fade-in for text generation.

### Settings
- **Intended Emotion**: Utility & Organization
- **Emotional Intensity**: Very Low
- **User Should Feel**: "Everything is exactly where I expect it."
- **Primary User Action**: Toggle preferences.
- **Motion Style**: Utilitarian. Standard slides and toggles.
- **Typography Style**: Standard UI scale.
- **Color Usage**: Slate accent (`#64748B`). Strictly neutral.
- **Content Density**: Compact. List views.
- **Microinteraction Style**: Crisp toggle switches.
